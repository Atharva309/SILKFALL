City Colours

Building Lights is how many windows are lit; Light Brightness is how bright the windows, signs and lamps are; Neon Glow is how much they bloom. City Lights looks like a real city at night: mostly white and yellow. Natural gives every light its own colour. Rain goes from a light drizzle to a downpour; slide it all the way up for snow. Haze drifts patches of mist across the city: the drone flies through thick ones and thin ones. Neon Edges sets how many buildings are outlined in neon. The time uses the Clock settings below.
